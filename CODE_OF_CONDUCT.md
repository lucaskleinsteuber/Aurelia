# Code of conduct

Everyone is welcome to contribute to Aurelia. Treat other people with respect,
discuss the work on its merits, and give feedback that someone can act on.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, and sharing someone else's
private information without permission are unacceptable. This applies to issues,
pull requests, reviews, and other project spaces.

Report conduct concerns privately to the [repository maintainer](https://github.com/lucaskleinsteuber)
using the contact details they make available. Include links and a brief account
of what happened. The maintainer may remove content, close discussions, or restrict
participation as needed to protect contributors.
